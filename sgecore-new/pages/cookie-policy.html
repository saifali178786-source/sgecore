1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies may also be used to remember preferences and support website functionality.

2. How Cookies May Be Used

Cookies and similar technologies may help keep the website functioning, remember preferences, understand website usage, and support services you choose to use.

3. Essential Cookies

Essential technologies support core website functions, such as security, navigation and account-related features. These functions may not work correctly if essential technologies are disabled.

4. Optional Cookies

Where used, analytics cookies may help us understand how visitors use the website. Marketing cookies may be used to support relevant communications or campaigns. Optional categories should only be used in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

5. Managing Your Preferences

You can use the Cookie Settings option in the website footer to save your optional preferences. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when our website or cookie practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
