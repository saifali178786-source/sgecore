Risk Disclosure

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. General Risk Warning

Digital assets and related activities involve risk. You should carefully consider your financial circumstances and risk tolerance before making any decision involving digital assets.

2. Price Volatility

Digital asset prices may rise or fall significantly over short periods. You may lose some or all of the value of an amount committed to a digital asset.

3. Mining and Performance Risk

Mining-related activity may be affected by network difficulty, operating costs, equipment performance, market prices, service interruptions and other factors. Displayed estimates or rates are not guaranteed returns.

4. Liquidity and Withdrawal Risk

There may be delays, limits, technical interruptions or market conditions that affect the ability to transfer, exchange or withdraw digital assets.

5. Technology and Cybersecurity Risk

Online platforms, devices, networks and digital wallets may be exposed to cyberattacks, software failures, unauthorized access and other technical risks.

6. Blockchain Transactions

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible once confirmed. Sending assets to an incorrect address or using an unsupported network may result in permanent loss.

7. Regulatory and Tax Considerations

Rules relating to digital assets may differ between locations and may change. You are responsible for understanding any legal, regulatory and tax obligations that apply to you.

8. No Financial Advice

Information on this website is not personal financial, investment, legal or tax advice. You should obtain independent professional advice where appropriate.

9. Only Risk What You Can Afford to Lose

Do not commit funds that you cannot afford to lose. Past performance, displayed figures and estimates do not predict future results.
