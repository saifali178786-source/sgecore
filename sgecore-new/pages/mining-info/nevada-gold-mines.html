Nevada Gold Mines

Explore the Nevada gold mining region, its operations, and the SDE CORE plan options associated with this listing.

About Nevada Gold Mines

Nevada Gold Mines is one of the world's major integrated gold-mining operations, located across northern Nevada in the United States. Established in July 2019, the operation brought together important mining assets previously associated with Barrick and Newmont. The joint venture is owned 61.5% by Barrick and 38.5% by Newmont.

Rather than operating as a single mine, Nevada Gold Mines is a connected network of mining complexes, processing facilities and supporting infrastructure. Its operations are organized around major districts including Carlin, Cortez, Turquoise Ridge and Phoenix. This integrated structure allows different types of gold-bearing ore to be processed through suitable facilities.

Location and Mining Region

The mining operations are spread across northern Nevada, a region known for its long history of gold exploration and production. Nevada has developed a substantial mining industry supported by geological expertise, established infrastructure, skilled workers and specialist processing facilities.

The region includes a variety of mineral deposits and geological formations. These deposits require careful exploration, geological assessment and mine planning before commercial extraction can take place.

Mining and Extraction Operations

Nevada Gold Mines uses a combination of open-pit and underground mining methods. Open-pit mining involves removing surface material to access mineral deposits, while underground operations use tunnels and specialized equipment to reach deeper ore bodies.

The selection of a mining method depends on factors such as deposit depth, ore geometry, rock conditions, mineral grade and operational economics. Both methods require detailed engineering, safety procedures and ongoing geological monitoring.

Gold Processing and Infrastructure

After extraction, gold-bearing ore is transported to processing facilities for treatment. Depending on the characteristics of the ore, processing may involve crushing, grinding, milling, flotation, roasting, pressure oxidation or heap-leach treatment.

The operation's interconnected processing network provides flexibility in handling different ore types. This infrastructure plays an important role in converting mined material into gold-bearing products that can be further refined.

Technology and Operational Management

Large-scale mining requires continuous coordination between geological teams, mining engineers, equipment operators, processing specialists and maintenance personnel. Modern mine planning uses geological information, engineering models and operational data to support extraction decisions and improve efficiency.

Equipment maintenance, worker training, production monitoring and safety management are essential parts of maintaining reliable mining operations.

Environmental Responsibility

Responsible mining requires attention to water management, energy consumption, waste handling, land disturbance and environmental monitoring. Mining operators must follow applicable regulations and environmental permits throughout the life of their operations.

Rehabilitation planning and ongoing engagement with local communities are also important elements of responsible resource development. These activities help address the environmental and social considerations associated with large-scale mining.

Why Nevada Gold Mines Is Notable

Nevada Gold Mines is notable for its combination of multiple mining districts, open-pit and underground operations, and extensive gold-processing infrastructure. Its integrated operating model demonstrates how several mining assets can work together within a coordinated regional mining complex.

Its long operating history and established mining infrastructure make northern Nevada an important part of the United States gold-mining industry.
