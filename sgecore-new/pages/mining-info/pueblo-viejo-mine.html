Pueblo Viejo Gold Mine

Discover one of the Caribbean's major gold-mining operations, recognized for its large mineral resources, modern processing facilities and significant role in the Dominican Republic's mining industry.

About Pueblo Viejo Gold Mine

Pueblo Viejo Gold Mine is a major gold-mining operation located in the Dominican Republic. Situated in the Sánchez Ramírez province, it is one of the country's most important mineral-producing operations and is internationally recognized for its large gold deposit and complex processing infrastructure.

The operation is associated with extensive investment in mining development, ore processing and supporting infrastructure. Its development has helped establish the Dominican Republic as an important gold-producing country in the Caribbean region.

Location and Mining History

Pueblo Viejo is located near the municipality of Cotuí in the Sánchez Ramírez province, approximately 100 kilometres northwest of Santo Domingo. The surrounding region has a long history of mineral exploration and mining activity.

Gold and other mineral deposits in the area were identified through geological exploration. The modern development of Pueblo Viejo involved extensive engineering, environmental planning and the construction of large-scale processing facilities. Commercial production from the modern operation began in 2012.

Geology and Mineral Resources

The Pueblo Viejo deposit is known for its substantial gold and silver mineralization. The ore body contains complex mineral associations that require specialized extraction and processing methods.

Geological exploration and resource modelling help determine the location, quality and characteristics of mineralized zones. Drilling, sampling and laboratory analysis support mine planning and decisions about extraction and processing.

Open-Pit Mining Operations

Pueblo Viejo uses open-pit mining methods to extract mineralized material from its deposits. This involves removing overburden and extracting ore through a sequence of carefully engineered mining benches.

Heavy machinery, drilling equipment and haulage trucks support the movement of ore and waste material. Mine engineers coordinate extraction schedules, equipment deployment and transportation to maintain safe and efficient operations.

Ore Processing and Gold Recovery

Pueblo Viejo is particularly known for processing refractory gold ore. This type of material can contain minerals that make conventional gold recovery more difficult and require additional treatment before gold can be recovered efficiently.

Processing operations include crushing, grinding and specialized treatment stages. Pressure oxidation is used to treat refractory ore, helping prepare the material for subsequent gold recovery processes.

Following treatment, further processing and refining stages are used to recover valuable metals. Process monitoring and laboratory testing support quality control and recovery performance.

Processing Infrastructure and Technology

The mine relies on extensive industrial infrastructure, including processing plants, material-handling systems, power facilities and water-management infrastructure.

Specialized processing technology is an important feature of the operation because of the complex characteristics of its ore. Engineering improvements and equipment maintenance help support production and operational reliability.

Environmental Management and Safety

Mining operations in the Dominican Republic require careful management of water resources, tailings, waste rock, land disturbance and surrounding ecosystems. Environmental monitoring and management systems are important parts of responsible operations.

Operational safety includes equipment inspections, worker training, monitoring of mine slopes, emergency response planning and controls across extraction and processing activities.

Economic and Industrial Importance

Pueblo Viejo is an important contributor to the Dominican Republic's mining industry. Its development has supported industrial infrastructure, technical employment and related services in the surrounding region.

The operation demonstrates the engineering and investment required to develop complex mineral deposits and process refractory gold ore on a large industrial scale.
